Drawing Order Simulator

Drawing order in p5.js

p5.js paints like a painter: each line adds paint on top of what is already there. Nothing is ever moved behind. Reorder the lines, then step through them and watch the layers stack up.

Try this in by yourself

  • In the house example, move circle() (the sun) below triangle(). The sun now sits in front of the hill. Ask students why.
  • Move background() to the last line. Everything disappears. Step through it so students see the shapes being painted and then covered.
  • Move the door's fill() below its rect(). The door turns the house colour. fill() changes the paint for the shapes that come after it, never before.
  • Turn off the first background() with //. The empty areas show the checkerboard: an unpainted canvas is transparent.
  • In the target example, ask students to predict the picture if the smallest circle is drawn first. Then try it.
  • In part 3, choose "In setup()". The circle leaves a trail because nothing paints over the old frames.
