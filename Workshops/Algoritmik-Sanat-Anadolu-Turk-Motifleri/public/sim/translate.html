translate() Simulator

How translate() works

translate() doesn't move the shape. It moves the point (0, 0), and every shape after it is drawn from there.

Drag the blue numbers, or the dots on the canvas.

Ideas for class
  • Ask where the rectangle lands before you change a number, then drag it.
  • Set a translate to negative values. The origin can sit outside the canvas.
  • In "Moves add up", step through line by line. The second translate starts from where the first one ended.
  • In "push() / pop()", ask where the green rect would go without pop(). Then step through to check.
  • Hover the canvas: the same pixel has two addresses, one before and one after translate().
