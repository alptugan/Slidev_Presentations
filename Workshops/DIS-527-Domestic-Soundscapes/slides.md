# R. Murray Schafer's Triad
### Foundational Framework (The Tuning of the World, 1977)

<div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-6 items-stretch mt-1.5">
  <div class="flex flex-col justify-between gap-2">
  <div class="p-2.5 px-3 rounded-lg border border-slate-200 dark:border-slate-800 bg-slate-50 dark:bg-slate-900/50" v-click>
  <div class="flex items-center justify-between">
  <h4 class="text-xs font-bold text-neutral-900 dark:text-neutral-100 m-0">Keynote Sounds</h4>
  <span class="text-[0.65rem] uppercase font-mono tracking-wider font-semibold text-slate-500">01 / Ground</span>
  </div>
  <p class="text-[0.75rem] text-neutral-600 dark:text-neutral-300 leading-snug m-0 mt-0.5">
  Continuous background hums outlining a space's acoustic character. Heard subconsciously.
  </p>
  <div class="mt-1 text-[0.68rem] font-mono text-slate-500 leading-tight">
  E.g., Fridge hum, HVAC (Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning) drone

  </div>
  <p class="text-[0.75rem] text-neutral-600 dark:text-neutral-300 leading-snug m-0 mt-0.5">
  Foreground acoustic events consciously attended to, conveying alerts, warnings, or cues.
  </p>
  <div class="mt-1 text-[0.68rem] font-mono text-amber-600 dark:text-amber-400 leading-tight">

# Bernie Krause's Soundscape Taxonomy
### The Ecological Triad (Krause, 2008)

<div class="max-w-[62%] mt-5 space-y-3.5">
  <!-- Biophony & Geophony side by side in 2 columns -->
  <div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-3.5">
  <div class="p-3.5 rounded-xl border border-emerald-500/40 bg-black/65 backdrop-blur-md shadow-2xl" v-click>
  <div class="flex items-center gap-2 mb-1.5">
  <span class="w-3 h-3 rounded-full bg-emerald-400 inline-block shadow-sm"></span>
  <h3 class="text-base font-bold text-white m-0">Biophony</h3>
  </div>
  <p class="text-xs text-neutral-200 leading-relaxed mb-2">
  Non-human biological vocalizations and organic sounds produced within an ecosystem.
  </p>
  <div class="text-[0.72rem] font-mono text-emerald-300">
  Pets, caged birds, houseplants settling, nocturnal insect chirps.
  </div>
  </div>

  <div class="p-3.5 rounded-xl border border-cyan-500/40 bg-black/65 backdrop-blur-md shadow-2xl" v-click>
  <div class="flex items-center gap-2 mb-1.5">
  <span class="w-3 h-3 rounded-full bg-cyan-400 inline-block shadow-sm"></span>
  <h3 class="text-base font-bold text-white m-0">Geophony</h3>
  </div>
  <p class="text-xs text-neutral-200 leading-relaxed mb-2">
  Naturally occurring physical sounds originating from non-biological environmental forces.
  </p>
  <div class="text-[0.72rem] font-mono text-cyan-300">
  Rain on skylights, wind through window sills, thunder vibration.
  </div>
  </div>
  </div>

  <!-- Anthrophony as single column underneath both -->
  <div class="p-3.5 rounded-xl border border-rose-500/40 bg-black/65 backdrop-blur-md shadow-2xl" v-click>
  <div class="flex items-center gap-2 mb-1.5">
  <span class="w-3 h-3 rounded-full bg-rose-400 inline-block shadow-sm"></span>
  <h3 class="text-base font-bold text-white m-0">Anthrophony</h3>
  </div>
  <p class="text-xs text-neutral-200 leading-relaxed mb-2">
  All acoustic signatures generated by human beings, electromechanical systems, and tools.
  </p>
  <div class="text-[0.72rem] font-mono text-rose-300">
  Appliance motors, speech, cutlery clatter, media streaming, footsteps.
  </div>
  </div>
</div>

# Extended Framework: Spatial Scales

  <span class="text-xs font-mono font-bold text-neutral-300 uppercase">Level 1</span>
  <h3 class="text-base font-bold text-white mt-1 mb-2">Distal Soundscape</h3>
  <p class="text-xs text-neutral-200 leading-relaxed mb-3">
  The objective spatial and temporal propagation of all sounds across the neighborhood and urban fabric.
  </p>
  <div class="text-[0.72rem] font-mono text-neutral-300 border-t border-white/10 pt-2">
  Highway drone, construction 3 blocks away, distant emergency sirens.
  </div>

  <h3 class="text-base font-bold text-white mt-1 mb-2">Proximal Soundscape</h3>
  <p class="text-xs text-neutral-200 leading-relaxed mb-3">
  The acoustic field that actually arrives at the physical threshold or enters the recording microphone.
  </p>
  <div class="text-[0.72rem] font-mono text-blue-300 border-t border-blue-500/30 pt-2">
  Filtered through walls, double glazing, door seals, and interior curtains.

  <p class="text-xs text-neutral-200 leading-relaxed mb-3">
  The subjective, affective, and cognitive interpretation by the resident listener.
  </p>
  <div class="text-[0.72rem] font-mono text-purple-300 border-t border-purple-500/30 pt-2">

# Sensory Ethnography in the Home
### Principles & Positioning (Sarah Pink)

<div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-8 items-center mt-2">
  <div>
  <ul class="space-y-3 text-sm text-neutral-700 dark:text-neutral-300 m-0 p-0 pl-4">
  <li v-click><strong>Embodied listening:</strong> Sound is experienced through the entire body—vibrations through floorboards, pressure against eardrums, thermal sensations.</li>
  <li v-click><strong>Routines & material culture:</strong> Everyday sounds are inseparable from physical domestic tasks (chopping, washing, opening curtains).</li>
  <li v-click><strong>Reflexivity:</strong> The researcher's physical presence actively alters the home's soundscape; your footsteps become part of the recording.</li>
  </ul>
  </div>

  <div class="p-5 rounded-xl border border-blue-200 dark:border-blue-800 bg-blue-50/40 dark:bg-blue-950/30" v-click>

  <p class="text-xs text-neutral-700 dark:text-neutral-200 leading-relaxed m-0">
  "Audio recording captures acoustic waveforms; sensory ethnography captures what those waveforms mean to the people living within them."
  </p>
  <div class="mt-3 text-[0.75rem] text-neutral-500 dark:text-neutral-400 font-mono">
  Always triangulate audio with spatial photos, floor layouts, and reflexive diary notes.

# Step 3: Recording Protocols & Ethics
### Equipment, Placement & Consent in Private Quarters

<div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-6 items-center mt-3">
  <div class="space-y-3">
  <div class="p-3.5 rounded-xl border border-white/15 bg-black/75 backdrop-blur-md shadow-2xl" v-click>
  <h4 class="text-xs font-bold text-blue-400 m-0 uppercase tracking-wide">Technical Settings</h4>
  <p class="text-xs text-neutral-200 m-0 mt-1.5 leading-relaxed">
  Record in <strong class="text-white">48 kHz / 24-bit uncompressed WAV</strong>. Maintain sufficient headroom (peaks around -12 dB to -6 dB) to avoid sudden clipping from clattering dishes.
  </p>
  </div>

  <div class="p-3.5 rounded-xl border border-white/15 bg-black/75 backdrop-blur-md shadow-2xl" v-click>
  <h4 class="text-xs font-bold text-blue-400 m-0 uppercase tracking-wide">Decoupling & Isolation</h4>
  <p class="text-xs text-neutral-200 m-0 mt-1.5 leading-relaxed">
  Never place microphones directly on tables or floors. Use shock mounts, rubber decoupling pads, or suspended stereo bars to eliminate mechanical rumble.
  </p>
  </div>
  </div>

  <div class="p-4 rounded-xl border border-red-500/40 bg-black/80 backdrop-blur-md shadow-2xl" v-click>
  <h4 class="text-xs font-mono uppercase tracking-wider font-bold text-red-400 mb-2.5">Ethics of Domestic Infiltration</h4>
  <ul class="text-xs text-neutral-200 space-y-2 m-0 p-0 pl-4 leading-relaxed">
  <li><strong class="text-white">Informed Consent:</strong> Every household member must sign consent forms detailing audio privacy.</li>
  <li><strong class="text-white">Right to Redact:</strong> Provide an explicit "pause recording" switch or post-session deletion rights for intimate speech.</li>
  <li><strong class="text-white">Data Anonymization:</strong> Low-pass filter or redact identifiable telephone numbers, addresses, and sensitive dialogues.</li>
  </ul>
  </div>
</div>
