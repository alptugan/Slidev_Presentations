COD 412 Sound & Media Active Syllabus

COD 412 Sound Projects & Media

Explores sonic art, acoustic ecology, data sonification, live coding, and audiovisual synesthesia. Integrates historical avant-garde sound practices with modern algorithmic audio engines, sensors, and spatial listening environments.

Live Coding & Algorithmic Music

  • Kinetic sound sculptures and procedural composition

Data Sonification

Week 07 Available

Listening Modes & Sonic Epistemology

  • Historical theories of audition and perception
  • Michel Chion's three listening modes: causal, semantic, reduced
  • Pierre Schaeffer's acousmatic listening phenomenon
  • Barry Truax's acoustic communication framework
  • Bernie Krause's biophony, geophony, and anthrophony taxonomy
