# COD 323 Assignment 03

a. Create a complete sound design for a short video, incorporating the diegetic, non-diegetic, and trans-diegetic layers of sound.
- **Video Selection:** Select, shoot, or generate a video scene that is a maximum of 20-30 seconds long. The scene should provide an opportunity for both natural (diegetic) and stylized (non-diegetic/trans-diegetic) sound elements.
- **Diegetic Sound:** Design a full and convincing soundscape for the scene using diegetic sounds (sounds that
originate within the world of the video, like footsteps, dialogue, or environmental noises).
- **Non-Diegetic or Trans-Diegetic Sound:** Incorporate at least one significant sound effect that is either non-diegetic (e.g., a musical score, stinger, or mood-setting sound not heard by the characters) or trans-diegetic (e.g., a sound that crosses between the world of the characters and the world of the audience, like a character's internal monologue or a highly stylized sound that represents a thought or memory).

b. Export the video with sound in mp4 format. Keep the video resolution 1920x1080 maximum. [Tutorial](https://teaching.alptugan.com/tutorials/davinci-resolve---video-export)
