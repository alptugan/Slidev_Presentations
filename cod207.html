COD 207 Creative Coding I

Introduction to computational thinking, algorithmic composition, and visual programming using JavaScript and p5.js. Explores the expressive possibilities of generative art, procedural drawing, interaction design, and autonomous agents.

Institution Özyeğin University
Department Communication Design

Week 04 Available

Loops, Motion & Moiré Patterns

  • Transforming position, scale, and style of multiple objects with minimal variables
  • Computational Thinking as a generator for visual algorithms
  • Optical phenomena: Moiré interference patterns in code
  • For loops, nested loops, and procedural grid generation

Week 05 Available

Generative Art Paradigms: Randomness & Repetition

  • What is Generative Art? Historical definitions & philosophy
  • 1950s–60s early computer art: Computers and Automation Magazine
  • Deterministic vs. Stochastic randomness
  • Controlled chaos in generative compositions
  • Case study: Vera Molnar, Désordres (1974)

Media & Images
